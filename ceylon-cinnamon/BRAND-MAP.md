## 3. POSITIONING

### Positioning statement
```
For adults 50+ who keep an eye on their blood sugar, and the family members who
keep an eye on them,
who are tired of scare-tactic supplements and labels they can't make sense of,
[BRAND] is the true Ceylon cinnamon daily
that makes supporting healthy blood sugar* a calm, simple habit you can check for yourself.
Unlike the softgel clones and the "doctor letter" ads,
we show the real milligrams, the batch lab report and real customers —
and we never use fear to sell.
```
\* Structure/function claim. Always carries the FDA disclaimer.

### The "only-we" test
| Claim | Could a competitor say it today? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "True Ceylon cinnamon" | Yes, all of them | Category ticket, not a differentiator |
| "Real milligrams on the label" | Resilia, Clarova, TrueQuill | Shared; necessary but not ownable alone |
| "Published batch lab reports" | TrueQuill | Shared with one small player |
| "We never use fear to sell" + real founder + no personas | No one at scale; their ad systems are built on the opposite | **Ownable** |
| Calm everyday-habit tone + caregiver voice | No one | **Ownable** |
| Sugar-free vegan gummy, DTC *(if format chosen)* | No one in the DTC set | **Ownable** |

**The ownable core = honesty you can check + calm + habit.** "Real numbers" is how we prove it, not the whole position.

### Category frame
"Ceylon cinnamon daily" — not "blood sugar pill", not "diabetes supplement". It keeps us on the shelf the buyer already searches ("ceylon cinnamon", §2) without sounding like medicine.

### Headline promise (hero options, test all three)
1. **"Ceylon cinnamon, with nothing to hide."**
2. **"A calmer way to support healthy blood sugar.\*"**
3. **"The daily cinnamon you can actually check."**

### Tagline candidates
- "True cinnamon. True numbers."
- "Every day. Nothing hidden."
- "The with-dinner habit."

## 4. WHO WE TALK TO

Hypotheses, to be checked against reviews and comments of competitors (customer-research skill) before scale.

### Persona A — the user: "Carol, 61"
- Retired teacher or still working; type 2 or prediabetes, or a doctor who said "let's watch it".
- Takes 3–6 pills a day already. Reads labels. Has seen the scary Facebook letters and half-believes them, half feels manipulated.
- **Wants:** to feel she is doing something sensible every day; to bring good numbers to her next check-up without making it her whole life.
- **Fears:** being sold another miracle; wasting money; interacting with her medication.
- **Her words (hypothesis):** "one more thing to take", "is this the real cinnamon?", "I don't want to be scared into buying", "my doctor said watch it".
- **Where:** Facebook feed, Facebook groups, YouTube, email.

### Persona B — the buyer: "Jess, 38"
- Carol's daughter (or a spouse). Researches for her parent, often buys and ships to them.
- **Wants:** something safe and legit she can feel good giving; easy to set up and forget (subscription shipped to the parent's address).
- **Fears:** buying her mom a scam product; side effects.
- **Her words (hypothesis):** "I want to help without nagging", "is this legit?", "what does the lab report say?".
- **Where:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook; more mobile, more comparison-shopping.

## 5. BRAND PLATFORM

- **Purpose:** take the fear and the fine print out of everyday health habits.
- **Promise:** a real Ceylon cinnamon daily, with every number shown and nothing hidden.
- **Values**
  1. *Show the receipts* — label, lab report, founder, real reviews; if we can't prove it, we don't say it.
  2. *Calm over fear* — we inform; we never scare.
  3. *Small habits count* — one daily step, explained plainly.
- **Personality:** the friend who is a pharmacist — warm, plain-spoken, a little dry-humoured, never preachy.
- **Archetype:** Sage + Caregiver (knowledge offered kindly). Competitors play Rebel ("what doctors won't tell you") or Hero-saviour; we deliberately do not.

### Format-specific proof layer
| | Gummy (CJ candidate) | Softgel (current store) |
|---|---|---|
| Hero fact | 200mg Ceylon extract (10:1 ≈ 2,000mg bark) + 100mcg chromium per 2 gummies, sugar-free, vegan, 60 days | 600mg extract (12:1 ≈ 7,200mg bark) in MCT oil, 30 days |
| Extra story | Pill fatigue: "the one you'll look forward to"; sugar-free, 25 calories | Absorption with MCT oil; one-a-day |
| Honest caveat to state | Lower extract dose than softgels; sweetened with sugar alcohols (maltitol can raise glucose slightly and upset stomachs in some people) | Gelatin shell (if confirmed) — never call it plant-based |
| Gap it fills | G5 + G7 strongly | Competes head-on with ~10 clones |

## 6. MESSAGING ARCHITECTURE

### Master narrative (≈90 words)
> Most cinnamon in American kitchens is Cassia, and most cinnamon supplements are sold with fear: scary letters, "nurses" who don't exist, numbers that don't add up. We think supporting healthy blood sugar* should feel calm and simple. So [BRAND] is true Ceylon cinnamon, the kind from Sri Lanka that's naturally very low in coumarin, made into a daily you take with a meal. We print the real milligrams, publish the lab report for every batch, and show you who's behind it. No scare tactics. Just a good habit you can check for yourself.

### Pillars (each needs 2+ proof points before it runs in ads)
| Pillar | Message | Proof points (status) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Real cinnamon** | Ceylon only (Cinnamomum verum), Sri Lanka, naturally low coumarin. | Supplier spec sheet [NEEDED]; species ID / DNA or HPTLC test [NEEDED]; Cassia-vs-Ceylon explainer (have) |
| **2. Nothing hidden** | Real milligrams on the front, the equivalence explained in one sentence, lab report per batch. | Label photo (after private label) [NEEDED]; batch COA incl. coumarin + heavy metals [NEEDED]; QR code to report [NEEDED] |
| **3. Calm, everyday habit** | One daily step with a meal; supports healthy blood sugar levels already in the normal range.* | Directions on label (have); habit content / reminders email flow [TO BUILD]; customer reviews after launch [LATER — never fabricate] |
| **4. Real people behind it** | A named founder, a real support inbox, a straight guarantee. | Founder story + photo [NEEDED]; support email/phone [NEEDED]; written guarantee terms [NEEDED] |

### Audience variants
- **To Carol:** "You read labels. So do we." · "No scary letters. Just the facts and a good habit."
- **To Jess:** "Helping your mom with her numbers shouldn't mean trusting a fake nurse on Facebook." · "Ships to her door every month. You can see the lab report first."

### Objection handling
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Is this the real cinnamon?" | Ceylon only, here's the species on the label and the batch test. |
| "Why 2,000/7,200mg if it's 200/600mg?" | One sentence: "It takes X mg of bark to make Y mg of extract. We show both." |
| "Will it work?" | No promises of results. It supports healthy levels already in normal range,* as part of diet and activity. Guarantee covers the risk. |
| "My medication?" | Ask your doctor first, especially for blood sugar medication. Said plainly, on the page and the label. |
| "Another subscription trap?" | Cancel in two clicks, reminder email before every charge. |

## 7. VOICE & COMPLIANCE LEXICON

**Voice:** warm · plain · confident · lightly witty. Short sentences. Second person. Explain, don't sell.

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| "supports healthy blood sugar levels already within the normal range*" | "lowers blood sugar", "reverses diabetes", "drops A1C" |
| "supports healthy glucose metabolism*" | "replaces your medication", "doctors hate this" |
| "Here's the lab report." | "Clinically proven" (unless a study on *this* product) |
| "Talk to your doctor." | "Your doctor won't tell you…" |
| "Real customers" (only after launch) | "100,000+ customers", "selling out fast", invented ratings |
| "The with-dinner habit" | "Miracle", "secret", "ancient cure", "kidney repair" |

Every page and ad with a benefit line carries the FDA disclaimer. No medical personas, no lab-diary stories, no before/after numbers. (Brief §8.)

## 9. VISUAL DIRECTION (brief for the designer)

- **Feel:** kitchen table, daylight, real hands, real food — not lab coats, not hospital blue, not gold-and-black "premium".
- **Colour — "the cinnamon pantry"** (applied to the store theme Nov 15, 2026). Inspired by cinnamon and what it pairs with: vanilla (the product's flavour), clove, the bark itself and the green leaf of the cinnamon tree. Avoid the red/urgent palettes of discount funnels.

  | Name | Hex | Inspired by | Use | Contrast |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Vanilla Cream | `#FBF6EC` | vanilla custard | main background | — |
  | Vanilla Bean | `#F1E4CF` | vanilla pod, toasted milk | alternate section background | — |
  | Clove | `#2E1C14` | whole clove | text, headlines | 15.1:1 on Vanilla Cream |
  | Cinnamon Bark | `#8E4220` | Ceylon bark | buttons, guarantee block, primary brand colour | 6.6:1 on cream; white text on it 7.1:1 |
  | Cinnamon Leaf | `#4F6B42` | leaf of *Cinnamomum verum* | accent: eyebrows, checks, step numbers, "Ceylon" highlights | 5.6:1 on cream, 4.8:1 on Vanilla Bean |
  | Ceylon Quill | `#D4A26A` | the pale rolled quill | decorative only (illustration, packaging, ad backgrounds) — never for text on light | 7.1:1 when used on Clove |

  Theme mapping: surface = Vanilla Cream, surface_2 = Vanilla Bean, on_surface = Clove, interactive = Cinnamon Bark, accent = Cinnamon Leaf.
- **Type:** a warm serif for headlines (store uses Playfair Display), a clean sans for body (DM Sans). Big, readable sizes for a 50+ reader.
- **Packaging cues:** the real milligrams large on the front; a QR "See this batch's lab report"; species name *Cinnamomum verum* on the front.
- **Never:** stock "doctor" photos, fake review widgets, countdown timers, competitor photos (including the CJ listing images — they appear to be another brand's product shots).
