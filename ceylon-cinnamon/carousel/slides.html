Not all cinnamon is the same

Ceylon vs. Cassia

Most supermarket cinnamon in the US is Cassia. We only use Ceylon.

CEYLON · WHAT WE USE
Cinnamomum verum
✓Thin, papery layers
✓Naturally very low in coumarin
✓Grown in Sri Lanka
CASSIA · MOST STORES
Cinnamomum cassia
–One thick, hard layer
–Much higher in coumarin
–Often not labeled by type
Tip: look for Cinnamomum verum on any label. If it just says "cinnamon", it's probably Cassia.
