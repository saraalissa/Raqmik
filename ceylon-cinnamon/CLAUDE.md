## 1. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

- A US direct-to-consumer (DTC) brand selling ONE hero product: a Ceylon cinnamon supplement positioned for blood-sugar support. The founder decided to enter this category on Nov 15, 2026, after deep research into the category leader (Metabolae).
- Primary build target: a Shopify store with a high-converting long-form PDP, plus advertorial/quiz funnel pages, About, FAQ, Guarantee and subscription management pages.
- Sales motion: paid social (Meta first) -> advertorial or PDP -> bundle/subscription offer. DTC first; TikTok Shop is a live secondary channel in this category (see §7).
- Stage: pre-launch. No brand name [TBD], no locked formula [TBD — the format fork in §3 is the first decision], no supplier [TBD], no inventory.
- This brand is SEPARATE from the founder's other projects (a UK magnesium gummies brand and others). Do not reuse their names, claims wording, or assets.

## 2. MARKET SNAPSHOT — WHY THIS CATEGORY, WHY NOW

- Google Trends (US, checked Nov 14, 2026): the search term "Ceylon cinnamon" is RISING and at its 5-year peak (index 100 in the week of Nov 8, 2026; ~2.7x its level of a year earlier). The generic term "cinnamon supplement" is FLAT (back to ~18% of its April 2026 spike). Interpretation: demand growth is real but is being CREATED by advertisers — people search for the product by brand name and formula ("ceylon cinnamon 7200mg equivalent with mct oil", "mhcp ceylon cinnamon", "metabolae", "resilia ceylon cinnamon"), not for the generic category. Whoever spends, owns the demand.
- The category's centre of gravity: US consumers aged ~50+, many with type 2 diabetes or prediabetes, who distrust their doctor's "watch and wait" advice. The hidden buyer is often the spouse/daughter buying for them.
- Commodity reality check (FACT): plain Ceylon cinnamon powder capsules cost the customer $0.03–$0.17/day on Amazon (NOW, Nutricost, Horbäach). Every DTC brand below survives ONLY on story, format (MCT-oil softgel), and guarantee. There is no product-moat in the ingredient itself.

## 3. THE STRATEGIC FORK — PICK BEFORE ANY BUILD

The market has split into two camps. The formula decision IS the positioning decision:

**CAMP A — The 7,200mg softgel clones.** One identical spec product sold by ~10 brands: 600mg of 12:1 Ceylon extract (= 7,200mg raw-herb equivalent) in MCT oil, 30 softgels per pouch, 1/day, $29.99–$39.99 with B2G1/B3G2 ladders and 30–90-day guarantees. Leader: Metabolae (§4.1). Joining this camp means out-executing clones on creative volume and trust signals, while carrying the camp's built-in attack surface (§8).

**CAMP B — The "real milligrams" attackers.** Cinnamon + berberine capsules (1,000mg Ceylon + 500mg berberine HCl per 2-capsule serving) at a $29.99 subscription price, marketed explicitly AGAINST Camp A: "we print the real milligrams on the front." Players: Resilia (§4.3 — by traffic the biggest site in the niche) and Clarova (§4.4). Joining this camp means a differentiated formula and a comparison/attack content engine.

**CAMP C — Clean-label premium.** Transparent labels, published lab results, no equivalence math, no BOGO theatrics (TrueQuill, Dear Apothecary — §5). Smaller, quieter, defensible; unproven at scale in this niche.

## 4. COMPETITOR DEEP PROFILES

### 4.1 Metabolae — the category's demand engine (FACT unless noted)
- Site: metabolae.com. Product: Ceylon Cinnamon 7,200mg softgels (600mg 12:1 extract of Cinnamomum verum, Sri Lanka, in organic MCT oil; MHCP + cinnamaldehyde mechanism story). Homepage headline: "Steady Blood Sugar. Steady Energy. Every Day."
- Traffic (SimilarWeb, Oct 2026): 3.005M visits, +44.2% MoM, 96% US, 1.648M uniques; global rank ~#25,514. Google Trends brand term: zero until April 2026, peak 100 on Sep 13, 2026, still ~81 in Nov 2026.
- Meta ads (verified via API, Nov 14, 2026): official Facebook Page (ID 941813742359623, 22.7K followers, created Mar 16, 2026) runs ~3,217 active US ads. A persona-page NETWORK (pages named as nurses/doctors — "Walker", "Carter", "Sandra Keller, RN", "John Tucker") runs thousands more; a keyword search for "Metabolae" returns ~18,600 active ads including that network — never cite the keyword figure as the brand's own. The network launched simultaneously around Jul 21, 2026. Persona pages are multi-brand affiliate shells (the same pages also advertise competitors' products; letter signatures mismatch the page names; credentials of the same persona vary between ads — a documented fabrication pattern).
- Creative system: essentially ONE image-ad concept on the official page (kidney angle, running since Aug 11–13, 2026, ~95 days); the network's engine is long-form advertorial LETTERS in primary text. Master letter template (15 beats): symptom hook -> persona credential -> personal lab-numbers crisis -> "watch and wait" system indictment -> two-compound mechanism (MHCP opens cellular glucose doors / cinnamaldehyde repairs endothelium) -> double disqualifier (Cassia is fake + dry powder doesn't absorb) -> spec block (DNA-verified Cinnamomum verum, Sri Lanka, 12:1, 7,200mg, organic MCT oil, third-party tested, made in USA, no coumarin) -> week-by-week lab diary -> doctor-says-"keep doing it" scene -> 90-day guarantee -> scarcity + official-site-only counterfeit warning -> P.S./P.P.S.
- Prices (FACT, Nov 14): Buy One $42.84 (list $59.99) · Buy 2 Get 1 $85.70 · Buy 3 Get 2 $128.56 · free shipping over $60 · Subscribe & Save extra 30% via Loop (on site, absent from ads). 90-day guarantee.
- Documented weaknesses (exploitable, all verified): fabricated personas with inconsistent credentials; inconsistent social proof (homepage "4.8/5 from 15,012+ reviews / 100,000+ customers" vs PDP "4.9/5 / 4,000+"); homepage says 60 softgels, product photo pouch says 30; clone/copycat domains (metabolae-official.store, metabolaee.com) siphoning its traffic; zero brand equity/community; fear + 67–70% discount-led selling. A third-party creator's $13.7–24.7M/month sales estimate is UNVERIFIED — do not use it.

- Identical product name/spec to Metabolae ("Ceylon Cinnamon 7200mg Equivalent with MCT Oil", 600mg 12:1, 30 softgels, gelatin). Prices: Buy 1 $34.99 (list $59.99) · B2G1 $59.99 · B3G2 $84.99. Shop app listing shows ~150 reviews; its Shop URLs carry Meta UTM parameters (paid social confirmed).

### 4.3 Resilia — resilia.shop (Camp B leader; biggest traffic in the niche)
- ⚠️ Resilia ≠ Resia. Different brands.
- Product: vegetable CAPSULES, not softgels — 1,000mg Ceylon cinnamon bark extract (standardised to cinnamaldehyde/polyphenols) + 500mg berberine HCl per 2-capsule serving; 60 capsules/pouch. Nothing else in the capsule.
- Prices: Subscribe & Save $29.99 (list $59.99) · one-time $39.99 · Buy 2 $59.99 with a free bamboo glass jar gift. 30-day money-back guarantee. Ships from the US; DTC only.

- Funnel: multiple long-form advertorials (a "Japan trip" story, a "7 things I learned" listicle), a blood-sugar quiz, a cited 2026 customer survey, US phone support. Operationally the most sophisticated competitor after Metabolae.

## 7. CHANNELS & INFRASTRUCTURE

- Meta: the demand-creation channel. Metabolae's official page alone runs ~3,200 active ads; the persona network multiplies that. Marketer teardowns (Oct–Nov 2026) single out this category as one of the heaviest-scaling Meta advertisers of the season — the clones draft behind Metabolae's spend.
- Native/display: Resilia's model (57.59% display traffic) — advertorial funnels fed by native networks.
- TikTok Shop (FACT): generic "Ceylon Cinnamon 7200mg Equivalent with MCT Oil" listings show real velocity (one storefront: 1.4K sold at $25.50, 4.5 stars), driven by no-name sellers + creator affiliates, not by the DTC brands. Creator unboxing/review content for the pouch format is active (Oct–Nov 2026).
- Grey market (FACT): the identical pouch sells unbranded and under names like Liraé/Resia on eBay from ~$7, including China-shipped listings using Metabolae's name. Counterfeit warnings are a standard trust element on clone PDPs for this reason.
- Listicle/review farms: kidneyresearchdaily.com (its "Top 5 kidney supplements" listicle funnels ALL buttons to Provela, an R-Alpha Lipoic Acid product — Metabolae appears as an unnamed photo at #3), bestcinnamonsupplement.com, globalhealthcaremagazine.com, dietaryhabit.com. Expect any new entrant to be "reviewed" by this ecosystem within weeks; Clarova has industrialised the pattern in reverse (comparison page as attack ad).

## 8. RISKS & COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS (US)

- Disease claims are the category's original sin: A1C drops, diabetes reversal, kidney-function improvements, and "doctor changed my medication" stories are DRUG claims. Metabolae's network and Living Root's LP make them; a class-action precedent exists in an adjacent category (Estroven, US suit filed Jul 2026). Our store: structure/function claims only, with the FDA disclaimer; no disease treatment/reversal claims, no fabricated lab diaries, no fake medical personas.
- The three attack lines any Camp A entrant will face from day one (already weaponised by Clarova): (1) the 7,200mg-equivalence math (600mg actual vs 7,200mg headline), (2) gelatin softgels marketed with plant-based language, (3) recycled/duplicated reviews. If Camp A is chosen, the label and PDP must pre-answer all three honestly.
- Never fabricate: reviews, customer counts, survey stats, doctor endorsements, "sold out" bars, or review-widget numbers. Several competitors do; it is their documented vulnerability, not a playbook to copy.
- Supplement basics: FDA-registered, cGMP manufacturer; Supplement Facts accuracy; coumarin/heavy-metal testing is a differentiator competitors already advertise (TrueQuill publishes batch reports).

## 9. BENCHMARKS FOR THE BUILD (what "good" looks like in this category)

- Offer ladder norms: single $29.99–$42.84; B2G1 ~$57–$86; B3G2 ~$82–$129; free shipping threshold ~$60; subscription discount 15–30% where offered. Guarantee norm: 90 days (30 is weak, 60 mid).
- PDP anatomy (from the leader): hero with dose on the front -> mechanism story (MHCP/cinnamaldehyde or the Camp B dual-pathway) -> Cassia-vs-Ceylon disqualifier -> spec/transparency block -> timeline expectations -> guarantee -> FAQ -> counterfeit/official-site notice. Advertorial letter + quiz funnels are first-class pages, not afterthoughts.
- Traffic is overwhelmingly mobile and paid-social-led; design mobile-first.
- Creative engine: longevity + replication is the category's success proxy (Metabolae's kidney image concept has run ~95 days; its letter template is replicated across dozens of ads). Plan for volume creative testing from day one.
