## Red flags

1. **The images look like another brand's Amazon listing.** Silver label with orange and green bands, the badge row and the infographic layout match the Horbäach (formerly Carlyle) Amazon style; the brand name area is cropped out. Likely either (a) the genuine branded product resold, or (b) an unbranded product listed with someone else's photos. **Do not use any of these images** on the store or in ads.
2. "Packaged & lab tested in the USA" and "GMP" are not verified for what CJ actually ships.
3. $2.28 for 120 gummies is far below what this product sells for on Amazon. Re-confirm price, and what is in the box.
4. Lower extract dose than the softgel camp (200 mg vs 600 mg extract) — position on format and honesty, never on "stronger".
5. Maltitol can raise blood glucose slightly and cause GI upset in some people: state carbs and sugar alcohols plainly for a blood-sugar-conscious buyer.
