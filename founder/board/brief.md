# MAHSA pH Lip Balm

- What it is: a clear pH color-changing lip balm that turns a sheer pink on the lips and is formulated to stay moisturizing, sold under the existing MAHSA brand (which already sells a Complexion Stick at mymahsa.com).
- Who it is for: women 50+ in the US who stopped wearing lipstick because "no color suits me", it dries their lips, or it bleeds into the lines around the mouth.
- What it sells, at what price: one balm at about $36 (planned range $34-$39, not final), a 2-pack at about $59 via a bundle app; later a bundle with the Complexion Stick. Free US shipping. 365-day money-back guarantee that also covers opened product. One-time purchase, no subscription.
- Where and how: online only (Shopify store mymahsa.com), customers found through Meta ads (Facebook/Instagram) with an ABO test campaign of three ad angles, plus UGC video. Product made by a contract manufacturer (not chosen yet).
- Budget and constraints: total budget before launch under $1,500 (first factory order, samples, photo/video, test ads). Store fixed costs $100-300 a month (Shopify plan, apps, domain), not counting ads. factory not chosen, unit cost unknown (working assumption $5 per unit incl. packaging, shipping ~$5 per order - both estimates). Positioning rule: honest claims only (no fake counters, no fake reviews, no anti-aging claims). The category is crowded with 14+ copycat brands selling the same Red 27 pH lipstick via advertorials and buy-1-get-1 offers.

## Context a stranger needs
- MAHSA already exists as a small Shopify brand for women 50+ (brand idea: "Your skin was never the problem"), selling a Complexion Stick. The lip balm would be its second product.
- Competitor facts checked on 2026-11-26 (pages opened): Kivora (the category leader) sells a pH lipstick at $39.99 with buy-1-get-1, claims 4.6/5 and 4.8/5 on its site while Trustpilot shows 3.8/5 from 1,283 reviews, lists no full ingredient list, and its 90-day guarantee excludes opened product. Areliaa sells at $19.97 buy-1-get-1 with 3,070 Trustpilot reviews (4.6/5); complaints are reapplication on dry lips, slow delivery, weak color. 14+ other copy brands (Fenvio, Relvie, Caelvya, Olmera, Nektar...) use the same advertorial template; prices mostly $19.99-$42.99; Olmera sells at $9.99 and is already openly honest ("4-6 hours of visible color", names Red 27). Nektar already markets "no subscription, ever". Fenvio has many complaints about a hidden $29.95/month subscription. Big brands: Charlotte Tilbury pH lip oil $26 (only 2 of 6 shades pH), TIRTIR $12.97 (contains fragrance), Winky Lux Flower Balm $12.60-$18 (breaks easily).
- Chemistry (Lab Muffin, cosmetic chemist): most pH lipsticks use Red 27 (CI 45410), which gives the same single cool pink on everyone; moisture triggers it. The "unique shade for you" claim used by competitors is not true.
- The founder's planned edge: honest claims, real hydration (not yet tested - no samples yet), full INCI, a guarantee that covers opened product, no subscription.
