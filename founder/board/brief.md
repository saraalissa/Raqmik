# Board brief: Mëva (stand-alone; everything you need is here)

## The idea
Mëva is a direct-to-consumer magnesium gummy brand for UK women aged 45–65 (England, Scotland, Wales), sold only on its own Shopify site (trymeva.co) and marketed with Meta ads on Instagram and Facebook.

## Product
- Mëva Magnesium Berry Gummies: small glossy deep-purple pyramid gummies, berry flavour, pectin-based (vegan), contain sugar and glucose syrup (not sugar-free).
- 2 gummies a day = 180mg magnesium as magnesium citrate = 48% of the daily reference intake. 60 gummies per jar = 30 days.
- Health claims allowed: only the 5 GB-authorised magnesium claims (e.g. "contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue", "normal psychological function", "normal muscle function"). No menopause or hormone claims.
- White-label stock product from a UK supplier (Nutribl / Troo Health Care), manufactured in Spain, packed in the UK. The same base product can be bought by other brands. Brand positioning: "the dose printed on the front, no hunting for the number" (label transparency).

## Offer and prices
- 1 jar £24.99, 2 jars £39.98, 3 jars £49.98 (3 jars = price of 2 single jars). One-off purchase, no subscription.
- Delivery Great Britain only, 2–3 working days, £4.99, free over £29 (so free from 2 jars).
- Guarantee: "Finish your first jar; if it's not for you, we refund it in full", claim within 45 days, first order only.
- Launch offer "Founding 500": 20% off the first order (single jar) for the first 500 waitlist members.

## Status and numbers so far (real)
- Pre-launch. Free waitlist on the site since 14 Nov 2026: 43 sign-ups by 22 Nov, almost all from Meta ads.
- Cost per waitlist sign-up (Meta spend / Shopify sign-ups): best ad about £4–5 on recent days, about £7–8 over its life; weaker ads £10–25. Current ad spend about £60–77 a day.
- The welcome email reached all subscribers; click rate 3% (old version).
- Unit economics estimate (after 20% VAT, product cost about £4.16 per jar incl. label, 3PL pick/pack/postage about £4.84–£5.70 per order, payment fees), before advertising: profit about £15 on a 1-jar order, £18.70 on 2 jars, £22.20 on 3 jars. With the Founding discount on 1 jar: about £11.
- Fixed costs: e-commerce platform about £25/month, food-business-operator labelling service £395/year, 3PL subscription free for 3 months then rising to about £50/week, data-protection fees about £170/year.
- First stock order planned: 50 jars (about £208 ex VAT). Repeat orders about 5 working days.
- Founder: solo, based outside the UK; owns the business through a US LLC (Organo LLC). UK VAT registration applied for (pending). No product liability insurance yet. Sales expected to open mid-December 2026 to January 2027.
