# Board summary: MAHSA pH Lip Balm

**Vote: 3 FUND IF, 0 FUND, 0 PASS. Average score 3.7 / 10** (Offers 4, Monopoly 3, Product 4).

The board applies published frameworks (summaries in `founder-board/lenses.md`); it is not those people. Full memos: `board/offers.md`, `board/monopoly.md`, `board/product.md`.

## Risks more than one member raised

1. **No proof yet, and the only real edge is untested.** No samples, no factory, no hydration test. Hydration is the only advantage that lives in the product itself (all three lenses).
2. **Everything else can be copied.** Same Red 27 dye as the category; honest claims already used by Olmera, "no subscription" already used by Nektar (Offers, Monopoly).
3. **The money is unknown.** Unit cost, MOQ, cost per customer and refund rate are estimates; an opened-product guarantee on a budget under $1,500 can drain cash (all three).
4. **Red 27 gives one cool pink**, which may repeat the "no color suits me" problem for some buyers (Product).

## Conditions (checklist for the rest of the pack)

- [ ] Test samples on women 50+ against a written pass bar (hydration, bleeding into lines, wearable pink, incl. deeper skin tones) before any order. Fail = do not launch. (Product, Offers, Monopoly)
- [ ] Build break-even from real factory quotes: unit cost, MOQ, shipping. (Offers) → `/founder-cfo`
- [ ] Measure the real cost per customer in a small test before scaling; set a stop-loss. (Monopoly, Offers) → `/founder-launch`
- [ ] Write and cost the guarantee (capped, e.g. one refund per customer) before the first ad. (Offers, Product) → `/founder-offer`, `/founder-cfo`
- [ ] Add an honest offer stack the buy-1-get-1 brands do not have. (Offers) → `/founder-offer`
- [ ] Launch one product with one sentence; defer the Complexion Stick bundle. (Product)
- [ ] Sell first to existing MAHSA customers; build an owned email/SMS list and published test results as the asset that compounds. (Monopoly) → `/founder-marketing`

## Where they disagree

- **Bundles:** the Offers lens wants a richer stack to justify the price; the Product lens wants fewer moving parts at launch (one product, bundles later).
- **Score:** Monopoly is the lowest (3) because nothing is proprietary; Offers and Product see a fixable proof problem (4).

## The strongest version the board can see

A single, tested balm sold first to MAHSA's existing 50+ customers, with published results from real testers (hydration and lip lines, on several skin tones), the full INCI and a capped guarantee that covers opened product. It wins on proof and brand trust inside a small niche, not on the dye or on price. This differs from the pitch: it delays paid Meta scaling and the bundle ladder until the samples pass and the real cost per customer is known.
