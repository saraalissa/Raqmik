# Pricing: MAHSA pH Lip Balm

Simulated buyers pick what to test; they are not proof.

1. **The price.** The panel's acceptable range is **$8.08 to $27.95** (median "getting expensive" $22, "too expensive" $32); $36 is above it, and so is the 2-pack at $29.50 a tube. Competitors (`competitors.md`): the copy cluster sits at $19.99-$42.99 list, but almost all sell buy-1-get-1, so the real price per tube is about $10-$21; Olmera $9.99; Charlotte Tilbury $26. The CFO shows the conflict: with paid Meta ads at an estimated $25 per order, one balm loses $3.94 at $36 and $11.94 at $28. **No single-balm price inside the buyers' range works with paid ads.** Recommendation: test **$28 for one** for existing MAHSA customers and organic traffic (contribution $14.09, no ad cost), and a **2-pack at about $49** ($24.50 a tube, inside the range) as the only paid-ads offer, then re-run the CFO with the real cost per order.
2. **The ladder.** One balm $28 · two for ~$49 (the one to push) · three for ~$65 (gift buyers). Bundle with the Complexion Stick later, as the board asked.
3. **The opening offer.** "Founding customers" price for the first batch only, with a stated end date; no fake "was $X" anchor.
4. **What to test with real buyers.** Two landing pages or a pre-sale to MAHSA's list: $28 single vs $49 2-pack, and $24 vs $28 single.
5. **The panel's price objections (verbatim).** "$36 for a tinted lip balm is more than I'll pay when I can get something similar for $10 to $20 at the drugstore." (P004) · "I can get a color-changing balm at Ulta for a fraction of that with a coupon." (P001) · "$36 is too much on a $29k fixed income, and the 2-for-$59 offer isn't the buy-one-get-one deal Gabriel waits for." (P012, paraphrased by the buyer agent)
