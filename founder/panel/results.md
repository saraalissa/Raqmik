## What would flip a no

- Seeing the full ingredient list right in the ad showing it's fragrance-free and gentle, plus a few real reviews from women over 50 with sensitive lips, and a lower intro price around $20.
- Seeing it at Ulta or Sephora where I could try it, or my granddaughter showing me real women my age using it on TikTok and saying it doesn't dry them out.
- A real deal like buy-one-get-one, plus honest reviews from women over 60 saying it stays moisturizing through the day and doesn't bleed into the lines.
- A real buy-one-get-one offer that brings it to about $18 a tube, plus some reviews from women my age.
- If it were sold at Ulta or a department store counter where I could try it, and my friends or real reviews said it truly healed dry, cracked lips better than my usual balm.
- Seeing the full ingredient list up front with no fragrance, flavoring or the usual irritants, plus a handful of honest reviews from women my age with sensitive lips saying it didn't dry or burn.
- Real reviews and before-and-after photos from women in their 60s, or a friend or my granddaughter's mom trying it first and telling me it's good
- Real reviews from women my age saying it doesn't dry out or feather, or a friend telling me she loves hers.
- Seeing the ingredient list right in the ad with no fragrance or flavoring, plus some real reviews from women my age with sensitive lips, and a first-order price closer to $20.
- Seeing reviews from women my age with sensitive lips saying it didn't irritate them, and the ad saying right up front that it's fragrance-free.
- A good number of real reviews with photos from women over 50 showing how the pink looks on older lips, ideally on Amazon or Trustpilot.
- A real price drop or buy-one-get-one under $25, plus reviews from women my age saying it actually doesn't dry out or bleed.
