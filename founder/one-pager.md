**Customer.** UK women aged 45–65 who shop on their phone and buy supplements several times a year. 43+ have joined the free waitlist since 14 Nov, almost all from Meta ads.

- a 7-day taste promise and a first-jar guarantee;
- sugar grams on the front and a published lab test;
- Founding 20% off any pack for 7 days, for the waitlist only;
- a first batch of 50 jars.
