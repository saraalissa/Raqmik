# Mëva competitors: research notes (UK, checked 22 Nov 2026)

Public web only. Prices are one-off prices for the comparable item on the day checked unless stated. "Elemental" means the magnesium figure the brand states per daily dose. Where a page could not be loaded, the figure comes from a search-engine snippet of that page and is marked "(snippet)".

**Mëva for reference:** 60 gummies / 30 days, 2 a day = 180mg elemental Mg (citrate), contains sugar + glucose syrup. £24.99 for 1 jar = **£0.83/day** (£1.00/day once £4.99 delivery is added, because 1 jar is under the £29 free-delivery line). 2 jars £39.98 = £0.67/day. 3 jars £49.98 = £0.56/day.

## 5. Substitutes (solving the problem without buying magnesium)
- **Food:** the NHS says women 19–64 need "270mg a day" and that "Magnesium is found in a wide variety of foods" such as spinach, nuts and wholemeal bread ([NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vitamins-and-minerals/others/)).
- **HRT or the GP route** for menopause symptoms such as sleep and mood ([NHS HRT](https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/hormone-replacement-therapy-hrt/)).
- **Sleep routine instead of a pill.** A nutritionist in [Marie Claire UK](https://www.marieclaire.co.uk/life/health-fitness/are-magnesium-gummies-good-for-sleep) says gummies won't fix sleep on their own.
- **A menopause multivitamin they already take** (e.g. Health & Her, Novomins Menopause £19.99, see [H&B](https://www.hollandandbarrett.com/shop/product/novomins-menopause-strawberry-flavoured-menopause-60-gummies-6100006993)), or doing nothing.
