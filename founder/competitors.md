## 2. Price range (30 days, direct gummies)
Lowest £16.99 · median about £19.49 · highest £29.99 (Health & Her, low Mg). Tablets: £5–9. Mëva at £24.99 is the 2nd most expensive gummy and 25% above Novomins for the same 180mg citrate dose.

## 3. Positioning map (axes: price per day × "made for midlife women")
- High price, midlife-branded: Health & Her (low magnesium).
- High price, generic: **Mëva today (but midlife-targeted in ads)**.
- Mid price, generic, high street: Novomins, Vitabiotics, H&B, Unfabled, Known Nutrition.
- Low price, generic tablets: Boots, Vitabiotics tablets, H&B tablets.
- Midlife-branded, non-oral: BetterYou Menopause spray/flakes.
Empty corner: **magnesium-only, full-dose gummy made for women 45–65**.

## 5. The gap
- **No magnesium-only gummy speaks to women 45–65.** Health & Her owns "perimenopause" but with ~56mg; the full-dose gummies are generic. Mëva's midlife focus is open ground (without menopause claims).
- **Honest one-off purchase, no subscription trap** answers a recurring complaint against three competitors.
- **Transparency** is only a partial gap: Novomins, Vitabiotics and Known Nutrition already print elemental mg; MagAsorb and Unfabled are unclear. "Dose on the front" must go further (sugar grams per serving on the front, full label page) to be distinctive.
- **No gap on price or product:** the same 180mg citrate gummy is sold for £16.99–£19.99 on the high street.

## 6. The threat
- **Novomins** already sells the same dose/form/format (180mg citrate, 2 a day, glucose syrup + sugar) at £19.99 / £14.99 subscribe, in Boots and H&B, with 347 reviews, heavy social ads and an existing Menopause range. It could copy a midlife positioning overnight.
- **Unfabled** (Boots, £16.99, 60 berry vegan citrate 180mg) may be the same or similar stock product.
- Sugar-free options (Novomins, Known Nutrition glycinate) make Mëva's sugar a visible weakness.
