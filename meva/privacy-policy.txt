What we collect
• Order details: name, delivery and billing address, email, phone number, what you bought.

Please don't send us details about your health. We don't need them to sell you our products.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis
• Process, deliver and support your order: contract.
• Keep tax and accounting records: legal obligation.
• Prevent fraud and keep our shop secure: legitimate interests.
• Send you emails about offers and new products: consent (you can unsubscribe at any time).
• Measure and improve our website and ads: consent, given through our cookie banner.

Who we share it with
We only share what each provider needs to do its job: our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment providers, our UK fulfilment partner and Royal Mail for delivery, our email service, and, if you agree to cookies, advertising and analytics platforms such as Meta and Google. We never sell your data.

International transfers
Some providers, and Organo LLC itself, are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where data leaves the UK we rely on safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US Data Bridge.

How long we keep it
Order and tax records: 6 years. Marketing data: until you unsubscribe. Account data: until you ask us to delete it, or after 3 years without activity.

Your rights
You can ask to access, correct, delete or move your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email info@trymeva.co and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies
We use essential cookies to run the shop. Analytics and advertising cookies are only set if you accept them in our cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time.

Children
Our products are for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
