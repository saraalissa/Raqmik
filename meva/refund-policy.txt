The no-drama guarantee, in one line

Finish your first jar. Not for you? We’ll refund it. Here’s exactly how it works, so there are no surprises on either side.

Who it’s for

• Your first order from trymeva.co only.
• One guarantee per person, per household and per delivery address. If someone at the same address, or using the same email, card or name, has already had a guarantee refund, the guarantee doesn’t apply again.
• Orders placed directly on trymeva.co. It doesn’t cover jars bought from anyone else.

How to ask

• Email info@trymeva.co within 30 days of your first order being delivered (we use the date on the tracking).
• Include your order number and the name and email used for the order.
• Optional: one line on why it wasn’t right. It helps us, but you don’t have to.

What we refund

• 1-jar order: we refund what you paid for the jar. Keep it or bin it. No need to post it back.
• 2- or 3-jar order: the guarantee covers one opened jar, which you don’t need to send back. For the other jars, you can either:
  
  • send them back unopened, with the seal intact, within 14 days of your email, and we’ll refund them too once they arrive; or
  • keep them, in which case they aren’t refunded.
  
  Jars that have been opened, or aren’t returned, can’t be refunded.
• The refund for each jar is what you actually paid for your order, divided equally between the jars in it (so any discount you had is included).
• Delivery charges and the cost of posting jars back aren’t refunded under the guarantee.

When you’ll get your money

• Refunds go back to your original payment method.
• For the opened jar: within 5 working days of us confirming your request.
• For returned unopened jars: within 5 working days of them arriving with us.
• Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.

What the guarantee doesn’t cover

• Second and later orders.
• Requests made more than 30 days after delivery.
• Repeat requests from the same person, household, address, email or card.
• Jars bought from other sellers.

We may decline a request where there’s clear misuse, for example several accounts used by the same household. We’ll always tell you why.

Your legal rights (these always apply)

The guarantee is extra. It doesn’t replace or reduce your rights under UK law.

• 14-day right to cancel: under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel any order within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. Jars must be returned unopened with the seal intact within 14 days of telling us. We refund the price of the jars and standard delivery within 14 days of receiving them. You pay the cost of posting them back. For health and hygiene reasons, jars that have been unsealed can’t be returned under this right.
• Damaged, faulty or wrong items: email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We’ll send a replacement or give you a full refund, including delivery, and you won’t pay for any return. This doesn’t affect your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

No subscriptions

Every order is a one-off purchase. Nothing renews and you’ll never be charged again unless you place a new order.

Returns address

Please email us before sending anything back and we’ll give you the returns address. Parcels sent without contacting us first may not be matched to your order.
