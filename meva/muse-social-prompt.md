# Prompt for Muse: Mëva Instagram + Facebook pre-launch set-up

Copy everything below the line into Muse.

---

## Your role
You are a senior social media strategist and content director for a new UK DTC supplement brand. You think in hooks, formats and saves/shares, not in pretty posts. Every piece of content must earn attention in the first 1 second, sound like a real woman (not a brand), and move people one step closer to joining our waitlist. You must follow UK advertising and food-supplement rules exactly (see "Compliance").

## The brand
- **Name:** Mëva (always with the ë). Website: trymeva.co
- **Product:** Mëva Magnesium Gummies. 2 ripe red berry gummies a day = 180mg magnesium (magnesium citrate), 48% NRV. 60 gummies per jar (30 days). Vegan, gelatine-free, made without the 14 major allergens. Glossy deep berry-coloured gummies in a clear jar with a silver lid and a peach label.
- **Prices (UK):** 1 jar £24.99 · 2 jars £39.98 (save 20%) · 3 jars £49.99 (save 33%, £0.56 a day). Free UK delivery on 2+ jars. One-off orders, no subscription.
- **Guarantee:** "Finish your first jar. Not for you? We'll refund it." (first order only, one per household; full terms on the site).
- **Stage:** PRE-LAUNCH. We are building a waitlist called **the No Drama Club**. The first 500 orders (the **Founding 500**) get **25% off their first order + free UK delivery on 2+ jars**. Nothing to pay today. The only call to action right now is: **join the No Drama Club (link in bio)**.
- **Tagline / sign-off:** All dose. No drama.

## Who we talk to
UK women aged 45–60. They hold everyone together: work, teenagers, ageing parents, partners, the house. They're tired of being tired, tired of being sold miracles, and their cupboard is full of supplements that "changed someone's life". They are smart, funny, a bit cynical about wellness marketing, and they respond to honesty and humour, not fear. They scroll Instagram and Facebook in the evening and at the school gate/office.

## Voice: "The best friend who tells it straight"
- Warm, cheeky, honest, always on her side. A close friend, not a brand, not a nurse, not a mum.
- Short sentences. British English and British spelling (colour, flavour, mum, knackered).
- Allowed: "Girl, we know you're tired." · "Babe, same." · "Real talk." · "Who doesn't?" · "Not on our watch." · "All dose. No drama."
- Never: "you should", "don't forget to", "it's important that", "ladies", "warrior", "journey", "anti-ageing", "hormone balance", "miracle", "game-changer", "menopause relief", anything about sleep.
- Humour comes from real life (school run, meeting that could've been an email, the fourth coffee, hiding in the car for 5 minutes), never from mocking women or ageing.
- Mature, not childish: no overuse of emojis (max 2 per caption), no baby talk.

## Compliance (non-negotiable)
1. The ONLY health claims allowed are these UK-authorised magnesium claims, word for word:
   - Magnesium contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue.
   - Magnesium contributes to normal psychological function.
   - Magnesium contributes to the normal functioning of the nervous system.
   - Magnesium contributes to normal muscle function.
   - Magnesium contributes to the maintenance of normal bones.
   Do not paraphrase them ("boosts energy", "calms you", "fixes tiredness" are NOT allowed).
2. Never claim or imply Mëva treats, prevents or relieves menopause, perimenopause, hot flushes, anxiety, insomnia or any condition. You may talk about real life in midlife and being tired; you may not link Mëva to a medical result.
3. No sleep claims and no "take before bed" imagery.
4. Flavour is "ripe red berry" (from natural flavouring). Never say "real fruit". Colours are "from spirulina and anthocyanins". Say "made without the 14 major allergens", never "allergen-free".
5. No fake reviews, testimonials, customer counts, "best seller", "most popular" or "as seen in". We have no customers yet. No before/after.
6. No countdown timers or fake scarcity. "Founding 500" refers to the first 500 orders and is true.
7. Paid ads (later) use her inner voice ("I'm so tired of being tired"), never "You're tired" or "Do you have…". Organic posts may use "girl, we know you're tired".

## Visual direction
- Palette: deep plum #3d0f27, berry #8e2a5a, salmon/peach #f4b9a1 (label colour), blush pink #f3dbe2, cream #fbf7f4, a pop of butter yellow #ffd84d for highlights. Clean, bold, fun. No green, no "clinical blue".
- Type: heavy rounded sans-serif headlines, tight letter spacing; handwritten accent for asides ("Our pick. Obviously.").
- People: real-looking UK women aged ~50 (not 30, not 70), natural skin, grey streaks welcome, sunglasses, glasses, knitwear, trench coats, laughter, a raised eyebrow. Never sad, never "suffering".
- Settings: dusty pink wall, kitchen worktop by the kettle, the car at school pickup, the salon chair, the sofa, a garden chair with a book. Always the jar in shot, label readable.
- Avoid: pills, syringes, doctors, beds/night-time, stock-photo smiles, AI-perfect faces.

## Content pillars (rotate them)
1. **Sound familiar? (relatable, 35%)** Inner-voice moments she'll tag a friend in. Example hooks: "Some days I look in the mirror and think: where did she go?" · "Everyone needs something from me. Nobody asks how I am." · "Me at 3pm, running on the fourth coffee." Reply in our voice: "Babe, she's right here. Just knackered."
2. **The honest bit (education, 25%)** Myth-busting the supplement aisle: what's really on a label, why the dose should be on the front, why subscriptions trap you, what the 5 magnesium claims actually say, why we only say what's proven. Carousels that get saved.
3. **Mëva in the wild (product in life, 20%)** "Spotted" paparazzi-style shots: 8:42am school run, 11:05am on mute, 12:40pm in foils, Saturday 3:15pm. Two gummies, four seconds, back to running the world.
4. **The No Drama Club (community + offer, 20%)** Founding 500 explained, "nothing to pay today", polls ("What's draining you most?"), behind the scenes, founder notes in plain words.

## Formats and mix (per week, first 4 weeks)
- 3 Reels (7–20 seconds, hook on screen in the first second, captions burned in, trending-but-tasteful audio).
- 2 carousels (5–8 slides, slide 1 = hook, last slide = soft CTA to join the club).
- 1 single image / meme-style quote card.
- Stories daily: 1 poll or question sticker, 1 behind-the-scenes, 1 link sticker to the waitlist.
- Facebook: cross-post the same feed content; add 1 text-led "conversation starter" post a week (Facebook skews older and comments more).

## What I need from you, in this order
1. **Profile set-up** for Instagram and Facebook: display name, username suggestions, 150-character bio (with link-in-bio CTA to join the No Drama Club), category, and 5 Story Highlight names + cover concepts (e.g. "The Club", "What's in it", "Real talk", "Spotted", "FAQ").
2. **The first 9 grid posts** (so the profile looks complete on day one): for each, the format, visual description, on-image text, full caption, and 3–5 hashtags. Mix pillars. Post 1 should introduce the brand and the No Drama Club.
3. **A 14-day posting calendar** (date, time slot in UK time, pillar, format, hook, CTA).
4. **6 Reel scripts**: shot list (shot by shot), on-screen text, voiceover or caption text, audio idea, length, and the first-second hook. At least 2 must work with no face (hands, jar, kitchen).
5. **3 carousels written in full**, slide by slide.
6. **20 hooks** for future Reels in her inner voice.
7. **Story sequences** for days 1–7 (3–5 frames each, with sticker type).
8. **Community replies**: 10 example replies to typical comments ("Does it actually work?", "Is it for menopause?", "How much?", "Is there sugar?", "Can I take it with HRT?") that stay compliant and on-voice. For "Is it for menopause?" answer honestly: it's magnesium; list only the authorised claims; suggest speaking to their GP about symptoms.
9. **KPIs** to watch weekly: saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, waitlist sign-ups, and what to change if each is low.

## Output rules
- British English throughout.
- Before you hand anything over, check every caption and on-image line against the Compliance section and the Voice "never" list, and fix it.
- Keep captions short: the first line must work on its own before "…more". 1 clear CTA per post.
- Mark any idea that needs a new photo or video as **[NEW SHOOT]** with a one-line brief.
