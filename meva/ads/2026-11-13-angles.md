# Mëva Meta ads: 3 angles × 5 concepts (13 Nov 2026)

## Brief

- **Destination:** the product page, with UTMs (`utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=waitlist_r1&utm_content=<concept id>`).
- **Objective:** custom conversion "Waitlist sign-up" (`customer_posted=true`).
- **Audience:** UK women aged 45–65+, using Original audience options (not Advantage+ audience).
- **Formats:** 4:5 (feed) and 9:16 (stories and reels). On 9:16, keep all text inside the 720×1200 safe zone.

**Grounding.** There are no customers yet, so there are no reviews, comments or winning ads of our own to work from. Every concept is grounded in one of:
- Sara's research files: the UK menopause Meta sweep (5 Nov), the Grüns/Nütrops deep dive (12 Nov) and the SPNutrition analysis (10 Nov). Longevity is the only performance proxy available.
- The Nutribl certificate of conformity PL-541.
- NDNS 2019–2023 Table 5.41.

**Rules applied to every concept**
- Health claims use only the GB-authorised magnesium wording, word for word.
- Nothing about sleep, hormones or menopause.
- No invented reviews, counts or urgency.
- Staged lifestyle scenes are never presented as customers.

**Headline test (on every concept)**
- H-A: `Join free: 20% off your first jar` (33 characters)
- H-B: `Reserve yours. Pay nothing today` (32 characters)

**Primary text** (the hook line changes per concept; the rest stays the same):

> [hook line]
> Mëva is two ripe red berry gummies a day with 180mg magnesium. Magnesium contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue.
> Join the No Drama Club for free. The first 500 get 20% off a single jar. Nothing to pay today.

## Angle A: "The dose is on the front" (transparency)

**Grounding.**
- SPNutrition's "check your bottle" machine: 3,500+ ads running since Mar 2025.
- Grüns puts its label and dose in every ad.
- Mëva fact: 90mg elemental per gummy, 24% NRV (CoC PL-541). Two gummies = 180mg, 48% NRV.

**Hypothesis:** Angle A gets a higher CTR than Angle B, because it gives a concrete, checkable reason to choose Mëva.

## Angle B: "Tired of being tired" (fatigue + the food gap)

**Grounding.**
- Vegetology "Can't fight the fog and fatigue?" ran 476 days.
- MylasMoss fatigue ad ran 212 days.
- Health & Her ran its "tired all the time" stat across 4 ads (77–80 days each).
- NDNS: 15% of UK women aged 19–64 are below the minimum (LRNI); average intake from food is 83% of the RNI.
- Mëva's fatigue claim is authorised word for word.

**Hypothesis:** Angle B gives a lower cost per sign-up than Angle A among women aged 45–55, because the problem is already felt.

## Angle C: "Zero risk, zero drama" (offer + risk reversal)

**Grounding.**
- Vegetology "Just £1 a day" ran 476 days.
- Nomend's guarantee ads ran 273 days.
- Vitabiotics Menopace "3 for 2, Free UK Delivery" ran 296 days, in several versions.
- Health & Her's "free" lead-magnet ads ran 77–80 days.
- Mëva facts: 3 jars £49.98 = 56p a day; single jar £24.99 = 83p a day; the guarantee as now on the site; FOUNDING20 is limited to 500 uses.

**Hypothesis:** Angle C has the highest landing-page conversion rate of the three, because the offer answers "what does it cost me?" before the click.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| C4 | FAQ card | **Q: What does the No Drama Club cost?** A: Nothing. And the first 500 get 20% off a single jar. | Joining is free. That's the whole catch. | Cream card with Q&A, small jar |
