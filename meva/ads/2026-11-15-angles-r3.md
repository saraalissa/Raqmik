# Mëva R3: 3 angles × 5 concepts (15 Nov 2026)

**Why these angles.** All 4 paid sign-ups came from the transparency ads (A4, A2, 2.1, 3.1). Of the women who answered "What's draining you most?", two picked "Not feeling like me" and one picked "Carrying everyone". Nobody picked "The 3pm slump". So:
- Angle 1 goes after the feeling.
- Angle 2 goes after the load.
- Angle 3 doubles down on the proven winner (honest label) with fresh executions.

**Rules for every concept**
- Health claims only in the authorised wording:
  - "Magnesium contributes to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue."
  - "Magnesium contributes to normal psychological function."
- No promise to make anyone "feel like herself again" or to fix mood.
- Nothing about menopause, hormones or sleep.
- Staged scenes are not presented as customers.
- No invented quotes or reviews. Quotes are written as the reader's own thoughts, not as testimonials.
- Destination: product page. CTA: Sign up. Description: "20% off your first jar".

## Angle 1: "Not feeling like me" (final, 16 Nov). All 5 approved; N2 hook = snooze.

**The psychology.** This isn't low mood. It's role engulfment: the self slowly replaced by roles (mum, carer, manager) until her own name, wants and time disappear.

The hooks use four mechanisms:
- **Hyper-specific recognition.** A small, oddly exact detail she's never said out loud, which triggers "that's me".
- **Invisibility made visible.** Showing what's absent: her name, her photos, her time.
- **Self-postponement.** "Later" as the quiet habit of putting herself last.
- **Permission, not fixing.** The product is never the cure. It's one tiny act of putting herself first.

**Meta rules.**
- Every line is first person ("I"), never "you are…" (the personal-attributes policy).
- No negative self-image used to sell.
- Claims are authorised wording only.
