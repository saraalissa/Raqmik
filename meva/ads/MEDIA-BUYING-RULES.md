# Sara's media-buying rules (from her video, 22 Nov 2026) — ALWAYS follow

## Testing campaign (ABO)
1. Before ANY decision, a test must spend at least 2–3× AOV (Sara prefers 3×) AND run at least 2–3 days.
   Exception: something clearly abnormal (e.g. CPC far above the other tests) → turn off early.
2. Did it spend 2–3× AOV?  No → keep it running, no decision.
3. Yes → did it get a sale?
   - No → turn it off and analyse why (CPC good or bad? hook / first second good or bad?) → test new versions.
   - Yes → raise budget +30% (not double), hold 1–2 days.
4. Did it get MORE PROFITABLE sales after the raise?
   - No → after ~2 days go back to the original budget, or turn off if numbers are very bad.
   - Yes → +30% again, every 1–2 days, while it keeps working.
   - The day after a raise is often weaker: give 2–3 days to optimise; react faster only if very bad.
5. After about a week of scaling: did it get at least 10 profitable sales?
   - Yes → duplicate the winning CREATIVE into the Scaling campaign. Do NOT turn it off in testing; keep scaling it there too (+30%). If both work, keep both.
   - No → wait until 10, or lower the budget, or turn off (depending on ROAS / cost per result).

## Scaling campaign (CBO)
- One campaign, ONE ad set, broad targeting (only who: women, where: GB, age), only winning creatives.
- Budget higher than tests (her example: from £500; can start £100–200 depending on budget).
- Every time a test reaches 10 profitable sales, add its creative here.
- Keep testing new creatives all the time to find new winners.
