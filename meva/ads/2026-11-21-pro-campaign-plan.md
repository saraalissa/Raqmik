# Mëva Meta — professional campaign plan (21 Nov 2026)

Source rules: ads skill (meta-decision-system). TCPL (target cost per sign-up) = **£6** (Sara, 21 Nov: waitlist stage, launch NOT on Black Friday). Review to £5 after 2 clean weeks. Derived thresholds: data gate £18 per ad (3×), watch £6–£9, replace > £9 (1.5×). Truth source for sign-ups = Shopify customer tags (ad-*), not the Meta pixel.

## Phase 1 (21–30 Nov): fix the signal, add testing
1. **Tracking (FIX, overrides everything).** Make every Shopify sign-up reach Meta as the conversion event, browser + server (Conversions API via the Shopify Facebook & Instagram app, or a custom pixel forwarding `meva_lead`). Target: Meta-counted ≥ 80% of Shopify sign-ups per day. Read-only diagnosis first; any change on the store/pixel only with Sara's OK.
2. **Testing campaign = R5** (CBO, £10/day, protected): C1, C2, C3, H6, H7. Day-7 delivery check; Stage-2 read at £18 spend per ad.
3. **Existing campaigns untouched** (R2 adset 1 £18, R2 adset 2 £6, R3 H £10, R4 CBO £16). No edits to live winners (edits reset learning).
4. Black Friday 27–30 Nov: waitlist only, no fake urgency, no price changes.

## Phase 2 (~1 Dec): consolidate into Scaling + Testing
- **MEVA | Scale | CBO** (~80% of budget): proven ads only, launched with their existing post IDs (keeps likes/comments): 1.4, G3b (label page), 1.1, H1/H3, 2.2 if still ≤ £6. Broad GB women 45–65.
- **MEVA | Test | CBO** (~20%): R5 + new concepts.
- Old R2/R3/R4 switched off only after the Scale campaign has delivered 3 days at ≤ £6.
- Graduation Test → Scale: ≥5 sign-ups, ≤ £6, ≥14 days live, ≥1 sign-up in last 7 days.

## Decision rules (every Monday, rolling 14 days, Shopify sign-ups)
- Spend < £18 → wait.
- £18+ and 0 sign-ups → stop the concept.
- CPL ≤ £6 → keep / candidate winner. £6–£9 → watch. > £9 → replace.
- Fatigue: frequency > 2.5 or CTR −20% vs baseline → start 2 new versions; > 4.0 → replace.
- Never pause without a replacement ready.

## Scaling
- +20% every 5 days on Scale CBO when CPL ≤ £6 for the prior 2 weeks and 3+ replacements are staged. Rollback: CPL > £9 after a step → cut 20–30%, hold 2 weeks.
- Every budget increase needs Sara's explicit OK.

## Creative pipeline (3–5 statics a week, image first)
- Iterate winners by hook first: 1.4 "no hunting for the dose" → new hooks on the same idea; G3b "gummy vs capsule" → other comparisons (powder, tablet, spray) to the label page.
- Destination test: 1.4 creative → label page vs product page (same creative, two ad sets).
- Rules: glossy gummies only (never sugar-coated), women 40–55 natural, only the 5 GB-authorised claims, no menopause/hormone claims.

## Cadence
- Mon: decision day (rules above) · Wed: launch new tests · Fri: scale/rollback · Weekly readout to Sara in Arabic (< 200 words).

## Launch switch (VAT number day)
- New Purchase-optimised campaign with the winning creatives; waitlist email to Founding 500 first; keep a small lead campaign only if the list is under 500.

## Black Friday week (26 Nov – 1 Dec)
- UK auction CPMs rise sharply; we have nothing to sell. Option: hold budgets flat (no scaling steps) and judge that week's CPL separately; do not launch new tests on 27–30 Nov.
