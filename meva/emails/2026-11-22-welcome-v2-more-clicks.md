Where: Shopify → Marketing → Automations → "Welcome new subscribers with a discount email" → open the email → edit.
Do NOT pause, rebuild or change the trigger. Keep the same design (burgundy header, peach hero, plum buttons).
Current click rate: 3% (42 sent). Goal: 8%+.

Why it should get more clicks:
1. A one-tap question right at the top (4 buttons). Polls are the easiest click there is, and the answers tell us which ad angle to write next.
2. A real button above the fold, with a curiosity reason to click ("see the real number"), instead of one text link at the very bottom.
3. A P.S. link at the end (P.S. lines are among the most-read parts of an email).
