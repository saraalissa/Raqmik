Where we deliver
We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland). We do not currently deliver to the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man or outside the UK.

Delivery costs and times
• Orders of £30 or more (2 or 3 jars): free Royal Mail Tracked delivery, usually 2 to 3 working days.
• Orders under £30 (1 jar): £4.99 for Royal Mail Tracked delivery, usually 2 to 3 working days.
• Next working day: £5.99 for Royal Mail Tracked 24, on any order.
Orders are packed and dispatched from our UK fulfilment centre, usually within 1 working day. Delivery times are counted from dispatch and are estimates; they can be longer at busy times or in remote areas.

Tracking
You'll receive an email with your tracking link as soon as your order is dispatched.

Lost or delayed parcels
If your parcel hasn't arrived within 7 working days of dispatch, email info@trymeva.co and we'll look into it with Royal Mail. If it's lost, we'll send a replacement or refund you.

Wrong address
Please check your delivery address carefully. If you notice a mistake, email us straight away and we'll try to update it before dispatch.
